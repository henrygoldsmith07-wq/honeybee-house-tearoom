Before you come

Common questions

Do I need to book?

For a pot of tea and a slice, walk in. For afternoon tea, a larger table, or anything with a birthday attached, book — the stand takes preparation and the tearoom would rather have it ready.

How much notice do you need for afternoon tea?

The tearoom confirms this when they reply, because it depends on the week and the size of the table. Book as early as you can and ask them directly on 029 2075 5380.

Can you do gluten-free or vegan?

Ask. Put it in the dietary box when you book and the kitchen will tell you honestly what they can manage for your date rather than promising something they can't.

Are children and pushchairs welcome?

Call to ask about access, pushchair space and seating before visiting.

Is there parking nearby?

Use the directions link to plan your route. Call to check access and nearby parking.
